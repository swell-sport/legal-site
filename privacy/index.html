Swell Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026. This policy describes Swell, bundle identifier com.swell.surfjournal.

Data on your device

Swell stores spot descriptions, observations, wave results, notes, imported photos, videos, voice recordings, exported reels, and your purchase ledger in the app container on this device. Swell has no account and no cross-device sync. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes that local copy.

Photos, camera, audio, and voice

When you tap a capture or import control, the system asks for permission to use the camera, the photo library, the microphone, or speech recognition. If you agree and allow access, Swell uses the camera to capture clips, the photo library to import photos you choose, and the microphone to record audio. Voice notes are transcribed on the device or by Apple speech services after you allow speech recognition. You may decline permission and keep using typed notes and manual wave logging.

Notifications

Swell asks for notification permission when you first open Journal. You may decline or change this permission in device Settings. Swell does not use advertising tracking permission.

Purchases

App Store purchases are processed by Apple. Swell keeps the transaction information needed to deliver Surf Reel Passes on this device. Swell does not sell your session data.

Third-party sharing

Swell does not send your photos, audio, or voice recordings to a third-party advertising network. When you choose Share, the destination you select receives the exported video under that service's own policies. Apple processes payments and, if you allow it, speech recognition.

Deletion

Deleting a session removes its private media and reels from the app. Clear All Data removes session content, preferences, and unused passes. A minimal deletion-time marker on the device prevents earlier transactions from granting passes again. Copies you already saved to Photos or shared elsewhere are not removed.
