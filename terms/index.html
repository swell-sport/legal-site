Using Swell

Swell records surf session observations and wave results that you enter, and creates session recap videos from media you provide. You are responsible for the accuracy of your entries and for having permission to use photos, audio, or other media you import or share.

Water safety

Swell does not provide forecasts, navigation, rescue, automatic wave detection, or safety assessments. A rip current acknowledgement only records your own observation. Do not use the app where looking at the device would distract you or create risk.

Export passes

Session recording, saved spot descriptions, editing, and previews are free. A successful standard 1080p export uses one Surf Reel Pass. A comparison or 4K export uses two passes in total, including when both are selected. Passes are consumed only after the finished reel is saved in Swell. Failed and cancelled exports do not consume passes. Saving or sharing the same existing reel again is free. Creating another final export is a new charged action.

Purchases and refund requests are handled by Apple. When a purchase is revoked, remaining unused passes from that purchase may be removed. Reels already generated stay on the device.
